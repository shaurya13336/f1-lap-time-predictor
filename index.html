05 — LEAKAGECONTROL THE BOUNDARYNO RANDOM SPLIT
05

Don't let the
future train itself.

Laps from a single stint are close neighbors. Randomly scattering them across train and test would let the model see the same tire story on both sides of the boundary.

BAD SPLITLEAKAGE RISK

Interleaved across the same stint.

TRAINsame stint
TESTsame stint
TRAINsame stint
TESTsame stint
TRAINsame stint

The model can memorize a nearby tire-age trajectory.

LEAKAGE-SAFE SPLITEVALUATION CONTRACT
